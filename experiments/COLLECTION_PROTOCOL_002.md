# Durable consumed-prefix collection controller

Frozen before any main policy-update outcome. `paired_collection.py` supplies
cache/journal orchestration, not a native model producer. Six engineering
controls use synthetic fixtures and do not contribute VLA efficacy data.
The later interruption-recovery control brings the total to seven; frozen
snapshot 003 preserves that revision before any main update outcome.

Each family/update has an immutable JSON context and separate directory.
Bind the exact suite manifest, evaluator/model/processor hashes, variant,
action cadence and cohort seed there before episodes. Scene seeds come from
the frozen state list; policy seeds are derived from the cohort seed, state
index and repeat, identical for old/new. Give families/updates separately
predeclared cohort seeds. No reference-cohort labels enter method allocation.

Methods receive only their requested ten-pair prefixes. The six gates retain
alpha=.05/(8*6), delta=.02, epsilon=.02 and the declared 1,000-pair cap.
Cache reuse shares outcomes across methods; physical union count and each
method's virtual consumption are distinct.

The producer must persist every attempted old/new episode, actions,
observations, reset/checker diagnostics, seed and model/evaluator identity
before returning a complete paired record. The controller checks strict
boolean outcomes, no episode error, exact reset, matched seeds and simulator
initial-state digest and complete reset-input digest (camera/robot observations,
scene XML, task/goal/checker/controller fields). The producer must report both
exact-state and exact-input checks. It hashes committed records, fsyncs atomic commits and
the consumed-batch journal, and reconstructs allocation on resume.

Incomplete outcomes are never false policy successes. Unresolved attempts,
changed context/evidence and torn journals block blind retries; preserve
the raw record and reconcile the incident explicitly. A cache commit that
exists without its completed attempt marker also requires reconciliation.
Totals of committed pair episodes exclude incomplete/error attempts and
cannot be reported as total physical cost. That denominator requires the
native producer's complete attempt log.
